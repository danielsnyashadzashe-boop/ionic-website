## Modularity as a commercial model, not just an architecture

Most ERP vendors describe themselves as modular and then price as a suite. The
practical result is a business paying for manufacturing planning it will never
run because it came bundled with the finance ledger it actually needed.

Every Ionic ERP module can be sold standalone. That is a statement about the
contract as much as the codebase. You can begin with one module, prove it, and
add the next when the business case exists rather than when the renewal date
arrives.

## Mobile-first is a claim worth testing

Plenty of enterprise software has a mobile app. Fewer are designed so that the
common actions (approving a requisition, checking stock, capturing a delivery)
are faster on a phone than at a desk.

That distinction matters most for the businesses Ionic ERP is built for:
growing organisations where the people who need to approve things are rarely
sitting in front of a back-office terminal.
