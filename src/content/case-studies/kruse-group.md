## The challenge

The Kruse Group runs Kruse Glass and Aluminum and Prestige Windows and Doors in
southern Alberta. Its commercial operation depended on a vendor it could not
easily leave, on manual processes, and on what a few key people knew.

## What we did

We mapped the entire business before designing anything. Then we built one
commercial operations platform covering CRM, tenders, estimating and take-offs,
scheduling, jobs, fabrication, logistics, fleet, assets, staff and HR, health
and safety, quality, finance and reporting. A mobile app lets crews work from
the field.

## The result

Vendor lock-in is gone, manual processes have been replaced, and knowledge that
used to live with individuals is built into the system. The residential side of
the group is next.
