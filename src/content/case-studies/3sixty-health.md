## The engagement

Healthcare administration is one of the few sectors where fraud, waste and abuse
are treated as a single category, because operationally they are hard to
separate. A claim that should not have been paid may be deliberate fraud, a
duplicate submitted in error, or a service delivered outside policy. The
financial effect is identical; only the response differs.

3Sixty Health needed to move from finding these after the fact to detecting them
as they occurred.

## What was deployed

A fraud, waste and abuse monitoring system on Ionic GRC, running alongside
business process discovery and optimisation across core operations.

Pairing the two was the substance of the engagement rather than a bundling
convenience. Anomaly detection is only as good as the understanding of what
normal looks like, and that understanding comes from process discovery. Deploying
detection without it produces a system that flags unusual-but-correct behaviour
and trains its users to ignore alerts.

## Why proactive is the operative word

The distinction 3Sixty Health drew was between detecting losses and mitigating
them, which is a question of timing rather than capability.

Retrospective analysis tells you what a loss cost. Monitoring that surfaces
anomalous patterns as they emerge gives you the option to prevent the next one.
The value is not in the report; it is in the interval between the pattern
appearing and someone being able to act on it.
