## The engagement

Fifty employees is an awkward size for expense management. Too many for a
finance team to process claims by hand without it consuming a meaningful part of
the month, too few to justify the enterprise platforms whose pricing and
implementation timelines assume a thousand.

National Video Vision also operates across South Africa and Canada, which turns
a routine reimbursement into a currency question.

## What deployment involved

ExpenseFlow was rolled out across the business, handling capture through to
reconciled reimbursement. AI receipt extraction removed the manual capture step;
policy-aware routing put each claim in front of the right approver rather than a
shared inbox.

The client's own emphasis fell on two things: multi-currency support and
integration that did not turn into a project. Both are properties of the
platform being designed for cross-border operation from the outset rather than
having it retrofitted for larger customers.

## Why this deployment mattered commercially

A 50-person business is a genuine test of whether a platform works without a
dedicated implementation team. Enterprise software frequently succeeds at scale
precisely because scale funds the specialists who make it work.

Deploying cleanly at this size is what makes the reseller route viable — and
that route is now the basis of two committed Canadian accounting-firm
partnerships.
