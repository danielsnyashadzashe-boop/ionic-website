## The challenge

Sirago is an underwriting manager within the Old Mutual Group. It was
transforming one process at a time, and each process needed months of analysis
before it reached an implementation-ready MVP. The programme had run past eight
months.

Requirements also lost meaning on the way from business teams to Genric, the
group company that builds and maintains Sirago's systems.

## What we did

We rolled out Process Genesis across Sirago so its own people could capture,
map and specify their processes. Genric adopted the Process Genesis development
module to deliver directly from those requirements.

## The result

Work that had taken eight months is now done in under one, per process. The
business owns its transformation, and business and IT work from the same maps
and requirements.
